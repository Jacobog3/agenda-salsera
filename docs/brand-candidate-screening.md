# Brand Candidate Screening

Screening date: 2026-08-09

This is a preliminary naming screen, not a legal trademark opinion and not a
guarantee that a domain or handle can be claimed. Availability changes until an
asset is successfully registered.

## Decision Log

### 2026-08-09 — First round rejected

The product owner rejected the first-round shortlist. BailaCerca must no longer
be treated as the leading recommendation.

The first round over-weighted functional clarity and digital availability. It
under-weighted distinctiveness, cultural resonance, emotional value, and the
ability to behave like a category-level master brand.

The recorded checks remain useful research, but none of the first-round names
should move into identity design or asset registration.

## First-Round Reference: BailaCerca

### BailaCerca

Why it initially passed the functional screen:

- communicates local discovery without naming a country;
- works for academies, classes, socials, workshops, and festivals;
- supports the product promise “discover where to dance and who to learn
  from”;
- is easy to pronounce and type in Spanish;
- is understandable enough for an international visitor with basic Spanish;
- works naturally in conversation: “lo encontré en BailaCerca”;
- can grow city by city without making Guatemala the parent category.

Initial asset checks:

| Asset | Result on 2026-08-09 | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `bailacerca.com` | No registration returned by the Verisign `.com` RDAP registry | High, but only registration secures it |
| Instagram `@bailacerca` | Direct profile URL reports that the profile is not available | Promising, but claimability is not guaranteed |
| YouTube `@bailacerca` | Direct handle URL returns 404 | Promising, but claimability is not guaranteed |
| TikTok `@bailacerca` | Public logged-out page did not expose a conclusive profile result | Must confirm by attempting the username during account setup |
| Facebook `/bailacerca` | Public logged-out page did not expose a conclusive profile result | Must confirm inside Meta account setup |
| Search collision | No material brand, dance platform, academy, or app collision found in the initial search | Requires formal trademark screening before major investment |
| App stores | No exact material collision found in the initial indexed search | Recheck directly before launch |

Why it was rejected as a master-brand recommendation:

- it sounds like a nearby-event finder or a product feature rather than a
  distinctive cultural brand;
- it emphasizes nearby discovery more than the relationship graph;
- it does not naturally carry premium festival profiles, artist histories, or
  an international dance ecosystem;
- the `Baila + utility` construction feels more like a campaign CTA than an
  authoritative platform;
- another party could register the available assets before selection;
- unavailable profile pages do not prove that a platform will release the
  handle;
- a formal trademark search is still pending.

Suggested descriptor:

> Academies, teachers, classes, and Latin dance events.

Suggested Spanish promise:

> Discover where to dance and who to learn from.

## Revised Marketing Criteria

Future candidates will be scored with these weights:

| Criterion | Weight | What it means |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Distinctiveness and ownability | 30% | Sounds like a brand, not a feature, campaign, or SEO phrase |
| Scope elasticity | 20% | Works for academies, artists, activities, festivals, cities, and future services |
| Latin-dance cultural resonance | 20% | Feels native to the community without being limited to one style |
| Memorability and phonetics | 15% | Easy to say, hear, remember, and repeat in Spanish |
| Digital availability | 10% | Practical domain and social-handle path |
| Descriptive SEO value | 5% | Helpful, but carried mainly by descriptors and page content |

Digital availability is a gate after strategic quality, not the source of the
name.

## Competitive Naming Benchmark

- **Where to Dance Salsa**: excellent search-query clarity, weak master-brand
  elasticity and limited to salsa.
- **LatinMap**: clear geographic utility, but cold and generic.
- **go&dance**: active and easy to understand, but generic and difficult to own.
- **Salsa Vida**: emotionally stronger because it joins a category with a human
  benefit, but it remains salsa-only.
- **DanceCompas**: the strongest strategic construction in the set because it
  combines the category with a culturally relevant word that also suggests
  rhythm and navigation.
- **Active Scene**: correctly frames an ecosystem, but lacks a distinctive Latin
  cultural signal.

The open positioning territory is a distinctive name that combines Latin-dance
culture with the idea of a living, connected scene. It should not simply say
“find dance near me.”

## Second-Round Direction

The next round should explore semi-invented or ownable names that evoke:

- a living scene;
- rhythm or musical structure;
- connection and circulation between people and places;
- culture rather than nightlife alone.

Strategically useful examples such as `Clave Viva` and `Danziva` demonstrate the
desired emotional territory, but they are not recommendations: their exact
digital assets have conflicts. `DanceClave` has a favorable `.com` signal but is
too structurally similar to DanceCompas and risks looking derivative.

### 2026-08-09 — Owner taste calibration

Reference brands selected by the product owner:

- Apple;
- Amazon;
- Nvidia;
- Gymshark;
- Google.

Shared naming characteristics:

- one compact master brand rather than a descriptive phrase;
- global pronunciation and strong visual word shape;
- metaphorical, unexpected, coined, or semi-invented naming;
- enough semantic distance to grow into multiple product categories;
- a strong symbol or story rather than a literal feature description;
- clarity supplied by product experience and descriptor, not by the name alone.

This changes the naming brief materially. The next candidate pool must avoid
obvious constructions built from `baila`, `salsa`, `ritmo`, `clave`, `mapa`,
`cerca`, `agenda`, or `Latam`. The target is a one-word global master brand with
either:

1. an unexpected real-world metaphor, like Apple or Amazon;
2. a defensible invented word, like Google or Nvidia; or
3. a vivid category-plus-symbol construction, like Gymshark, only if it is
   genuinely distinctive and not derivative of DanceCompas.

### 2026-08-09 — Audience and purpose calibration

The founder's professional background and preferred technology brands must not
drive the consumer brand. The intended audience is dancers, beginners,
travelers, academies, teachers, artists, organizers, and festival attendees.

The desired first impression is:

- clear rather than chaotic;
- accessible rather than insider-only;
- transparent rather than secretive;
- neutral rather than aligned with one academy or group;
- connective without pretending that rival organizations are one community;
- trustworthy enough to make practical decisions.

The naming strategy should therefore communicate an open, reliable organizing
layer for the scene. It should avoid both a cold infrastructure/startup identity
and the tribal language of any one dance group.

Searches also identified DanceSignal as a current nightlife/music platform that
connects users, artists, venues, and events. This reinforces the need to avoid
generic `Dance + network/discovery word` compounds.

## Recommended Decision Gate

### 2026-08-09 — Concrete recommendation pending owner approval

**OpenPista** is the current recommended master brand, with
`openpista.com` as the primary domain.

Why it fits the clarified promise:

- `Open` communicates accessibility, transparency, and a platform that is not
  controlled by one academy;
- `Pista` gives it an immediate cultural connection to social dance without
  restricting it to salsa, bachata, Guatemala, or events alone;
- it works as a platform name for permanent profiles, event editions, artist
  appearances, academies, and future transaction products;
- it is short enough to say, remember, and use as a compact master brand.

Availability signals checked on 2026-08-09:

- Verisign's official `.com` RDAP returned no domain record for
  `openpista.com`, so the domain appeared registrable at the time checked;
- the direct Instagram URL for `@openpista` showed no available profile;
- the direct YouTube handle URL returned `404`;
- the only exact-name web collision found was a very small open-source Rust AI
  agent project. It is outside the dance, ticketing, travel, and events
  categories, but it should remain in the final trademark/SEO review.

Suggested public descriptor: **Academias, artistas y eventos de baile latino.**

The recommendation is not considered secured until the domain is registered
and the social handles are successfully claimed.

### 2026-08-09 — Salsify and SalsaHub screening

Both names are rejected as master-brand candidates:

- **Salsify** has attractive coined-brand qualities, but it is the established
  name of a large international product-experience-management technology
  company using `salsify.com`. The collision is direct, prominent, and
  unacceptable for a technology platform.
- **SalsaHub** is immediately understandable but is too generic, restricts the
  platform to salsa, and uses the overused technology suffix `Hub`. Its exact
  `.com` has been registered since 2004 and is currently parked for resale.
  `salsahub.com.br` belongs to an unrelated Brazilian printing business, while
  `salsahub.co.uk` is already used for salsa dance education. These conflicts
  would make search ownership and international expansion unnecessarily hard.

If forced to compare only their linguistic quality, `Salsify` is more ownable
and memorable while `SalsaHub` is clearer. Neither is legally or strategically
viable for this product.

### 2026-08-09 — Salsa-led naming direction

The founder's proposals indicate that `Salsa` may be acceptable as an umbrella
brand for a broader Latin social-dance product, even when profiles and events
also include bachata and other styles. Under that assumption, the strongest new
candidate is **SalsaAtlas**.

1. **SalsaAtlas** — `salsaatlas.com`
   - strongest recommendation in this naming direction;
   - combines immediate category recognition with a global organizing metaphor;
   - supports cities, academies, artists, festivals, event editions, and travel;
   - the official `.com` registry returned no record on the date checked;
   - `@salsaatlas` showed no active Instagram profile;
   - exact-name web searches found no material dance or technology platform
     collision. A condiment product named “Salsa Atlas” exists in Ecuador, which
     is a minor cross-category collision.
2. **SalsaScope** — `salsascope.com`
   - compact and technology-oriented, communicating a complete view of the scene;
   - the `.com` returned no registry record;
   - weaker in spoken Spanish because `scope` is less universally understood.
3. **SalsaScout** — `salsascout.com`
   - strong for discovering events, academies, and dance travel;
   - the `.com` returned no registry record;
   - weaker as an organizer, profile-database, and ticketing platform because it
     sounds primarily like a consumer discovery tool.
4. **Salsora** — `salsora.com`
   - closest to the compact, coined quality the founder liked in `Salsify`;
   - the `.com` returned no registry record;
   - less self-explanatory and already appears as an unrelated fashion handle,
     so it is not a leading recommendation.

`Bailario` was rejected despite an available exact `.com`: a 2026 iPhone app
called Mai Bailario already offers dance-event discovery for salsa, bachata,
kizomba, zouk, and tango, creating a direct product collision.

### 2026-08-09 — Memorability and visual-read correction

The founder rejected `SalsaAtlas` because it is not contagious or immediately
easy to read visually. This is a valid change to the decision hierarchy: oral
transmission and instant recognition must outweigh conceptual completeness.

The strongest candidate under the revised rule is **Hay Baile**, presented as
`HayBaile` in digital contexts:

- domain: `haybaile.com` returned no record in the official `.com` registry;
- exact phrase searches found no material app or platform using the joined name;
- it creates a repeatable call-and-response: “¿Hay baile?” — “HayBaile”;
- it is style-neutral and works across cities and countries;
- it is short and can be displayed as two visually distinct words;
- it directly expresses the user's most common discovery question.

The exact Instagram handle `@haybaile` is occupied by a private, apparently
inactive account with zero followers. `@haybaileapp` showed no active profile
and is the practical launch handle unless the exact handle can later be
acquired. The phrase's generic nature also reduces inherent trademark strength,
so a formal trademark search and a distinctive visual identity are important.

`DaleBaile` was rejected despite an available `.com` because it is already the
name and social identity of a recurring São Paulo event. `HayBaile` currently
has the better balance of memorability, product relevance, and collision risk.

### 2026-08-09 — SoneroLatino screening

**SoneroLatino** is not recommended as the platform master brand:

- `sonerolatino.com` returned no record in the official `.com` registry;
- the exact Instagram handle `@sonerolatino` is occupied by “Sonero De
  Profesión”, an active identity with hundreds of followers;
- `sonero` conventionally identifies a singer or improvising performer in son
  and salsa, so the name sounds like a male artist, radio program, or salsa-music
  publication;
- it excludes or weakens the perception of female artists, dancers, bachata,
  academies, venues, organizers, and general event discovery;
- at six spoken syllables it is readable but not especially compact.

It has more cultural flavor than `SalsaAtlas`, but it points to the wrong
product category. It would be suitable for a salsa-music media project, not for
the neutral Latin social-dance infrastructure being built here.

### 2026-08-09 — Bailongo naming direction

`Bailongo` establishes a stronger emotional direction: colloquial, rhythmic,
memorable, style-neutral, and immediately associated with enjoying dance.
However, the exact master brand is not sufficiently ownable:

- `bailongo.com` is registered;
- `@bailongo` is occupied on Instagram;
- Bailongo Montréal is an active and established tango festival with a prior
  Bailongo mobile app;
- the word is a common colloquial term for a dance party or a person who enjoys
  dancing, which weakens inherent trademark protection.

Screened derivatives with no `.com` registry record at the time checked:

1. **MiBailongo** — `mibailongo.com`
   - leading viable derivative;
   - `@mibailongo` showed no active Instagram profile;
   - personal, warm, readable, and natural in “lo vi en MiBailongo”;
   - can support a personal agenda while still housing public profiles.
2. **SoyBailongo** — `soybailongo.com`
   - strongest identity and hashtag behavior;
   - weaker because the singular masculine adjective may not feel universal.
3. **SomosBailongo** — `somosbailongo.com`
   - inclusive and movement-like;
   - longer and risks implying a unified community despite academy rivalries.
4. **BailongoYa** — `bailongoya.com`
   - energetic and memorable;
   - too focused on immediate event discovery for the broader profile platform.
5. **Bailonia** — `bailonia.com`
   - compact invented “world of dance” idea;
   - minor but relevant entertainment collisions include a 2026 electronic
     track and an Italian theater name.

`MiBailongo` currently has the best balance if the founder wants to preserve the
`Bailongo` energy. It still requires formal trademark screening before purchase
or launch.

### 2026-08-09 — SomosSalsa screening

**SomosSalsa** becomes the current leading candidate, subject to formal
trademark review:

- `somossalsa.com` returned no record in the official `.com` registry;
- the exact Instagram handle `@somossalsa` is occupied by a nearly empty account
  with one follower;
- `@somossalsaapp` showed no active Instagram profile and is the practical
  launch fallback;
- the two five-letter words create a balanced, highly legible wordmark;
- four spoken syllables, repeated `s` sounds, and an existing natural phrase
  make it easy to hear, spell, remember, and repeat;
- it works as an identity statement and a neutral banner across academy
  rivalries: “Somos Salsa” belongs to the activity rather than one organizer;
- geographic extensions are natural: SomosSalsa Guatemala, México, Costa Rica,
  and so on.

Material risks:

- the name frames salsa as the umbrella and may make bachata-only or other-dance
  audiences feel secondary;
- the phrase is widely used in music, including albums and songs by established
  salsa artists, reducing inherent distinctiveness;
- the Mexican food brand Somos sells salsa products, creating unrelated search
  noise around the two words;
- a common phrase will require a distinctive visual system and careful trademark
  protection in the relevant software, event, media, and ticketing classes.

Despite those risks, `SomosSalsa` has substantially better memorability,
emotional participation, and visual balance than `SalsaAtlas`, `OpenPista`, or
the prior descriptive compounds. It also has a stronger movement-quality than
`MiBailongo`. The name should now lead the shortlist if the founder explicitly
accepts salsa as the umbrella for salsa, bachata, and adjacent Latin social
dances.

### 2026-08-09 — Digital asset decision

The founder secured the social handle **`@somossalsa.app`** and registered
**`somossalsa.com`** through AWS Route 53.

Both `somossalsa.com` and `somossalsa.app` returned `404 / not found` in their
respective official registry RDAP services when rechecked, indicating that both
appeared registrable at that moment.

Recommended domain architecture:

- register both domains in the same registrar account;
- use `somossalsa.com` as the sole canonical public website and email domain;
- redirect `somossalsa.app` permanently to `somossalsa.com`;
- do not duplicate or split content between the two domains;
- enable automatic renewal, account MFA, registrar lock, and WHOIS privacy;
- configure a valid TLS certificate before serving or redirecting `.app`, since
  the entire `.app` namespace requires HTTPS through HSTS preloading.

Final acquisition status on 2026-08-09:

- **Brand:** SomosSalsa
- **Primary domain:** `somossalsa.com`, registered through AWS Route 53
- **Defensive/app domain:** `somossalsa.app`, registered through GoDaddy
- **Social handle:** `@somossalsa.app`, secured by the founder

The essential naming assets are now secured. Registrar and DNS provider may
differ without affecting the product. After the initial 60-day transfer lock,
`somossalsa.app` may be moved to Route 53 if consolidation is still desirable,
but this is optional and not a launch dependency.

The local application rebrand may proceed because the essential naming assets
are secured. Public traffic should move only after the location migration,
production environment, contact channel, HTTPS redirects, and SEO migration
have been verified. A focused trademark review in Guatemala and the first
expansion markets remains advisable before investing heavily in promotion.
